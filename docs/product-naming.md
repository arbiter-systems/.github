# Arbiter Product Naming Guidance

Use public-safe product vocabulary in organization profile content, public docs, issue titles, PR summaries, demos, and UI labels.

## Public guidance

- Use names that describe user-visible outcomes.
- Avoid implying production readiness, compliance certification, billing-grade reporting, or customer deployment unless those claims are approved and currently true.
- Keep public examples synthetic and safe.
- Do not expose private naming documents, roadmap notes, competitive strategy, or internal planning records.

## Current public positioning

Arbiter Systems builds infrastructure for controlled, observable, reliable AI execution.
