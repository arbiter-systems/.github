# Milestone and Release Guidance

Detailed milestone, release tag, and readiness-gate strategy has been moved out of the public organization repository.

Public guidance:

- Keep public release language accurate and supportable.
- Do not imply production readiness, compliance certification, customer deployment, or service-level commitments unless approved and currently true.
- Do not publish internal checkpoint gates, private milestone sequencing, release-tag plans, or known-gap records.

Detailed release planning belongs in private operational records.
