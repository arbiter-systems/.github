# Branch Protection and Merge Guidance

Detailed branch protection and merge policy has been moved out of the public organization repository.

Public guidance:

- Keep changes pull-request based where practical.
- Use human review before merging.
- Keep changes small, scoped, and reversible.
- Do not publish internal ruleset details, required-check plans, bypass rules, admin verification notes, or repository-specific enforcement state.

Detailed merge policy belongs in private operational records or repo-local guidance.
