# Accessibility Review Guidance

Detailed MVP accessibility audit checklists have been moved out of the public organization repository.

Public guidance:

- Review public pages, docs, and screenshots for keyboard access, focus visibility, heading structure, labels, contrast, and clear error states.
- Do not publish internal demo readiness gates, private review worksheets, known deferrals, or product roadmap details.
- Do not describe accessibility readiness as certification or legal compliance unless that claim is approved and currently true.

Detailed accessibility review records belong in private operational records or repo-local guidance.
